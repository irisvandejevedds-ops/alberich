The golden mean

Alberich judges you by moderation, not abstinence.

BroodingYou barely spend. Nothing gained, nothing lost.
PleasedYou spend within reason. This is the goal.
AilingYou overspend a little. The coin-sickness takes him at once.
CursedYou overspend past your buffer. Recovery takes days of moderation.

Who is he?

Alberich means "elf-ruler", the root of Oberon. In the Nibelungenlied he guards the Nibelung hoard with the strength of twelve men until Siegfried takes the cloak of invisibility and pulls his beard. In Wagner's Ring he forswears love, steals the Rhinegold, forges the Ring, enslaves the Nibelungs, and curses the Ring when Wotan takes it. He is, in short, a hoarder with a temper. You are here to stop him becoming either extreme.
