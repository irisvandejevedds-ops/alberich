The Phone

The Alberich app on your phone keeps your pouches and taps on the phone itself. Pairing connects it to this computer.

The phone app

What it is

A small app ({{ apk_kb }} KB) with the pouches, Alberich, reminders and the home-screen widget. It works on its own, with no server. Paired with this computer, it brings in the credits worked out from your statements and keeps a copy of your taps here. It is signed with this computer's own key and is not on any store, so Android will ask permission to install from your browser the first time. Installing it over an older Alberich widget app keeps the widget but starts with empty pouches until you pair.

Pair a phone

How pairing works

Give the phone a name, make a pairing code, scan it with the phone's camera app and tap the link, or copy the link into the app under Settings. The code holds a secret key, so only show it to the phone you are pairing. It works over your home Wi-Fi, and over Tailscale if you use it. Cancel a key below to unpair that phone.
